Privacy policy

Unified Scheduler brings your calendars and tasks together on your Windows device. This page explains the personal information the app handles and the choices you have.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Unified Scheduler Windows desktop application provided through the project repository. It also describes this static project website. Google, your Moodle institution, GitHub and other third parties operate their own services under their own terms and privacy policies.

The app handles personal information, including calendar content stored locally. You can use local tasks and events without connecting an online account. Connecting Google or Moodle is optional.

2. Google Calendar access

When you choose Connect Google Account, the app opens Google's official authorization page in your system browser. You select an account and decide whether to grant access. Unified Scheduler does not ask for or receive your Google password.

The current build requests these permissions:

PermissionPurpose
openid
userinfo.email		Verify the Google account's identity and show its email address so multiple accounts remain distinguishable.
calendar.calendarlist.readonlyList the calendars you can access, including names, identifiers and display metadata, so you can choose which calendars appear.
calendar.events.readonlyRead events from enabled calendars for the configured synchronization window, including shared calendars accessible to your account.

Except for openid, the full scope names use the prefix https://www.googleapis.com/auth/. Earlier app versions requested calendar.readonly; existing grants may remain broader until you revoke access and authorize the current build again.

Calendar API responses can contain event titles, descriptions, start and end times, time zones, locations, links, identifiers, recurrence information and other event metadata, including organizer or attendee information. The app normalizes these responses and retains the event fields used for its schedule: identifiers, titles, descriptions, times, dates, locations, event links and local tracking/reminder settings. It does not persist a separate organizer or attendee directory.

Data is used to display your combined schedule, search and filter events, maintain an offline cache and show reminders. Google connections are read-only. Marking an imported event complete affects your local tracking state and does not modify Google Calendar.

3. Storage on your device

The app stores its workspace in a per-user application data directory. By default on Windows this is normally %LOCALAPPDATA%\UnifiedScheduler\Unified Scheduler; a custom data directory can be selected when launching the app.

  • SQLite workspace: local tasks and events, imported calendar content, account email and verified account identifier, calendar/source metadata, completion and reminder state, and settings.
  • Windows Credential Manager: Google access/refresh credentials and full private Moodle calendar subscription URLs. The app does not provide a plaintext credential fallback.
  • Application files: a release may include the developer's Google Desktop OAuth client configuration. This identifies the application; it is separate from a user's access and refresh tokens. A distributed desktop client cannot keep an embedded client secret confidential.

The SQLite calendar database is not encrypted by the application. Protect your Windows account, device and backups. Windows credential storage improves protection of tokens, but this policy does not guarantee protection against every form of device compromise.

Reminder notifications may display event titles or timing information through Windows. Whether they appear on a lock screen or to someone nearby depends on your Windows settings.

4. Moodle / MOLE calendar subscriptions

If you add a Moodle/MOLE calendar, you provide a private HTTPS calendar export URL. That URL can contain a token which grants access to the feed, so it is treated as a credential and stored in Windows Credential Manager. The app does not require your Moodle username or password.

The app downloads the feed directly from the configured calendar service and saves normalized events in the local workspace. Course access, the institution's export settings and the feed's date range determine which events are available. Marking a deadline complete in Unified Scheduler does not submit an assignment or change Moodle.

Do not post your private subscription URL in GitHub issues or screenshots. If it is exposed, use the institution's controls to replace or revoke it where available, then update the app's subscription.

5. Network requests and sharing

This implementation has no developer-operated synchronization server, analytics service, advertising SDK or automatic crash-report upload. Calendar content and account credentials are not sent to the developer by the app.

When you connect or synchronize, the app communicates directly with Google or your selected Moodle calendar service over HTTPS. Those services receive the requests and the credentials needed to fulfill them and may record connection information under their own policies. Google identity verification can also retrieve Google's public signing keys. Opening a calendar link in your browser sends a request to that link's destination.

This website contains no tracking scripts, externally hosted fonts or account login. GitHub Pages or another chosen website host still receives ordinary website requests, including connection information such as IP addresses. GitHub downloads, support issues and email messages are handled by their respective service providers. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

If you voluntarily email the developer or open a public support issue, the information you include is shared through that channel. Send only the information necessary for support; never include tokens, private calendar URLs, personal databases or unredacted calendar exports. Public issues are visible to other people.

6. Retention and your controls

Local tasks, settings and tracking state remain until you remove them or the workspace. Successful synchronization replaces each fetched calendar's cached events for the chosen date window. A failed synchronization keeps its previous cache, so old information can remain available offline. Hiding a calendar preserves its cache and connection.

  • Remove a Google account: use the Accounts page to disconnect it. The app deletes that account's locally stored credentials, calendar metadata, cached events and associated local completion/reminder state. Other sources and local tasks remain.
  • Revoke Google permission: use Google Account third-party connections. Local disconnection does not itself revoke the grant at Google, and remote revocation does not erase the app's existing local cache. Disconnect in the app as well when you want both removed.
  • Remove a Moodle subscription: remove its source in Accounts to delete its stored URL, cached events and associated tracking state. This does not delete events on Moodle.
  • Remove the whole workspace: first disconnect every online source in each workspace while its credential store is available. Then choose Quit from the tray menu and delete that workspace's data directory. Deleting the database alone does not delete its credential-vault entries. If the workspace was already lost, review Windows Credential Manager for entries beginning with UnifiedScheduler:, remove only those belonging to the app, and revoke the relevant Google grants.
  • Backups and copies: separately remove any backups or copied data directories you no longer need. Uninstalling or deleting a portable app folder does not necessarily remove per-user data, credential-vault entries or backups.

Deleting records is not a promise of forensic erasure from storage media or backups. The developer cannot directly access or erase a workspace that exists only on your device.

Google authorization can expire or be revoked. The app refreshes access tokens when Google permits it and asks you to reconnect when needed; it does not guarantee a permanent connection. Revocation or a failed refresh leaves cached events available until you remove the source.

7. Google data and Limited Use

Unified Scheduler's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google data is used to provide the calendar and planning features described here. This implementation does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. It has no developer-operated system for people to browse your calendar content. The publisher must maintain these commitments for future releases and review any new data use before introducing it.

This statement describes the intended privacy commitments and current implementation. It does not mean Google has reviewed, certified or approved the application.
