Using Unified Scheduler

Terms of service

A desktop workspace for your schedule, with optional connections to services you already use.

Effective October 31, 2026 · Publisher: Unified Scheduler

1. The application

Unified Scheduler is a Windows desktop calendar and task manager. It displays local plans, Google Calendar events and Moodle/MOLE calendar subscriptions in a combined workspace. Online connections are optional. The project is maintained under the name Unified Scheduler; contact cuizon.morell@gmail.com with questions.

These terms describe the intended use of the published application. The software's MIT license governs your rights to use, copy, modify and distribute its code. These terms do not remove those license rights or any rights applicable law does not allow to be excluded.

2. Your use and responsibilities

Use the app only with accounts, calendars and information you are authorized to access, and follow applicable law and your institution's or organization's policies. You are responsible for choosing the sources you connect, protecting your Windows device and keeping appropriate backups.

Do not share account tokens or private Moodle subscription URLs. Do not use the app to bypass Google's consent requirements, account restrictions or an institution's access controls. Sign in to Google only on its official page in your system browser; the app should never ask you to enter a Google password into its own interface.

3. Third-party integrations

Google, Moodle operators, GitHub and other external services are independently operated. Their terms, privacy practices, permissions, availability and account policies also apply. An integration can change, be restricted, stop working or require you to reconnect. Google publishing and verification status is controlled through the developer's Cloud project and Google's review process.

Google Calendar and Moodle integrations are read-only in this release. Local edits and completion tracking do not update remote events or submit coursework. The application is not an official product of Google or of your educational institution, and no endorsement is claimed.

4. Schedule and reminder limitations

Use your original calendar and learning platform to confirm important deadlines. Cached information can be out of date, and events outside the configured synchronization window or the Moodle feed's export range may not appear.

Reminders require the app to remain running, including in the Windows tray. Quitting the app stops reminders and background polling. Windows notification settings, device state and network availability can affect the experience. The app is not an emergency notification service and is not intended for situations requiring guaranteed delivery or timing.

5. Privacy and removing data

The privacy policy describes the information accessed, local storage, credential handling, external requests, retention and account controls. Calendar content is personal information even when it stays on your device.

You can stop using the app, disconnect sources and remove local data. Removing the portable application folder alone may leave its workspace and Windows credential entries behind; follow the privacy policy's removal instructions. Revoking a Google grant and deleting a local cache are separate actions.
